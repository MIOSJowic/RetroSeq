RetroSeq example songs
======================

Open a song with FILES (F) -> Load. Each track starts without an output:
put the cursor on the Output column and press Ctrl-O to choose a MIDI port
or a VST3 instrument. The Reference Manual (Start menu -> RetroSeq) uses
DEMO.mid in its tutorial and examples.

DEMO.mid
    16 bars in 4/4, 120 BPM: Piano (ch 1), Bass (ch 2), Drums (ch 10),
    Strings (ch 3) and Lead (ch 4). Lead plays bars 9-12 only; try
    adding empty bars and Loop (manual: View Screen, Track Length).

Waltz in 3-4.mid
    8 bars in 3/4, 150 BPM: Piano, Bass and Melody. Shows a song in
    another time signature.

Drum Loop - 4 bars.mid
    One drum track (ch 10), 110 BPM, a rock beat with a fill in bar 4.
    Use FILES -> Import to load it into a track of your own song, then
    turn on Loop.

Bass Line - 4 bars.mid
    One bass track, 110 BPM, made to play with the drum loop. Import it
    into another track.

Straight Hats - try swing.mid
    Two bars of straight 16th-note hi-hats. Mark the bars on the View
    screen, press X and choose Super-Quantize with grid 16 and swing 60
    to hear the difference.

All files are standard MIDI files and open in any sequencer or DAW.
